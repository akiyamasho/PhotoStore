# PhotoStore 0.20.5 — concurrent 4K playback and compact audio

**Status: implementation integrated; final production build and measured delivery pending.**

- Video rows are 48 points, with a thin attached waveform band inside the row. They no longer
  consume a separate Movie Audio row. Standalone audio has one editable waveform row. Mute and
  audio properties remain available; ordinary linked-band gestures do not detach source timing.
- A bounded waveform texture cache reuses images during playback and draws one image per visible
  waveform, avoiding cold source work and thousands of per-frame line segments.
- Decoded Rec.709 RGBA8 writes exact CMS ramps directly into tiles, removing a full-frame
  temporary/copy. Byte-compatible GPU tile uploads avoid conversion copies; dense changing
  movie pages use bounded page transfers while sparse edits/shared fills retain their paths.
- The host-sized worker pool uses actual successful thread counts, memory-based decode admission
  and the same eight-session/shared-memory ceiling. Decoder-session reservations reclaim optional
  cached frames before retiring warm streams; current requested frames remain pinned. No private
  memory preferences are increased.
- Video playback pre-roll holds the starting frame until decode and primary canvas upload/GPU
  completion are ready, then starts audio and its clock. Stop/seek/rebase discard old readiness
  signals; Frame Animation retains its existing timing path.
- `photostore-cli playback-bench --paths '["/path/source.mp4",…]' --frames 180 [--rate 30]`
  measures the production hardware compositor, decoded frames, GPU completion and paced deadlines,
  with a bounded final sample readback against CPU pixels. It never opens or records the app UI.

The [performance target and architecture](../plans/4k-performance-2026-11-01.md) define a bounded
eight-source 4K SDR/30 fps workload before publication. Final binary evidence will distinguish
original dimensions, cold startup, warm throughput and paced completion from native display/input/
sound acceptance. A successful decode-only benchmark cannot satisfy that target.

Built-in GPT Image [design](../design/2026-11-01/compact-4k/workflow.png) and
[exact prompt/provenance](../design/2026-11-01/compact-4k/prompt.md) preceded implementation.
The design's Auto quality control is conceptual; no measurement may silently lower resolution.
The image is synthetic, not a product screenshot. Original-quality export, editable Smart Objects,
native serialization and the previously published layer/audio ownership fixes remain authoritative.

Existing codec/HDR, Smart-transform, archive/load quotas and native input limitations remain as
documented in prior releases. Artifacts use the persistent local PhotoStore certificate, not
Developer ID/not notarized. The scheduler must stay disabled with no pending replay through the
single-source build, publication, atomic normal installation and documentation-only receipts.
