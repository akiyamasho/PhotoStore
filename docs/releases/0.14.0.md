# PhotoStore 0.14.0 — close safety and timeline persistence

Status: development milestone 3 of 3. Five of eight delivery steps are complete: version/docs,
preview, actual screenshot evidence, PhotoStore-dev isolation, and the development compiler gate.

stopwatches, or Photoshop parity.
