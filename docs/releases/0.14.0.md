# PhotoStore 0.14.0 — close safety and timeline persistence

Status: development milestone 3 of 3. Version/docs, preview, and actual screenshot evidence are
complete. The separate PhotoStore-dev identity/configuration is implemented; development compiler
packaging, development commit/push, exact-SHA development package, and background development
installation remain pending. No production tag or release is planned in this milestone.

The bounded pass covers close safeguards (including Cancel close-intent reset and removal of the
application-quit bypass) and selective timeline persistence for same-playhead post-edit video
surfaces and dense properties before history/save. It does not claim complete persistence coverage,
stopwatches, or Photoshop parity.

The required compiler check passed in 33.04 seconds. The preview build completed in 5m32s with
marker `preview-0.14.0-timeline-safety` on 2026-10-11. The native demo `.pcraft` is 8,314 bytes;
its ZIP manifest records generator `photostore-format 0.14.0`, frame 0 with one video tile, and
blank frames 1 and 2 before seeking. Targeted captures show the close guard retaining the document
after Escape and a reopened timeline/video thumbnail with painted frame data; the canvas is clipped
and no full painted stroke is claimed. Dense-property runtime behavior remains unverified.

The eight-step development delivery is: version/docs; preview; actual screenshots/docs;
development isolation; development compiler; development commit/push; exact-SHA development
package; and background development installation. Production packaging, publication, and
installation remain out of scope until explicitly requested.
